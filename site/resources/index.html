Reference & Resources

HF Band Plans

  • 160m: 1.830-2.000 MHz (CW/Digital), 1.900-2.000 (SSB)
  • 80m: 3.500-3.800 (CW), 3.800-4.000 (SSB)
  • 40m: 7.000-7.300 (CW), 7.300-7.500 (SSB)
  • 20m: 14.000-14.350 (CW), 14.350-14.500 (SSB)
  • 15m: 21.000-21.450 (CW), 21.450-21.550 (SSB)
  • 10m: 28.000-28.500 (CW), 28.500-29.700 (SSB)

Common Contest Frequencies

  • CW Contests: 3.505, 7.005, 14.005, 21.005, 28.005 MHz
  • SSB Contests: 3.845, 7.245, 14.285, 21.345 MHz
  • RTTY: 3.580, 7.080, 14.080, 21.080 MHz
  • FT8: 3.573, 7.074, 10.136, 14.074, 21.074 MHz

VHF/UHF Band Plans

  • 50 MHz: 50.000-50.300 (CW/SSB)
  • 144 MHz: 144.000-144.300 (CW/SSB), 144.300+ (FM)
  • 430 MHz: 430.000-430.500 (CW/SSB), 430.500+ (FM)

Troubleshooting Quick Tips

  • No CAT: Check port, baud rate, drivers
  • No PTT: Verify DTR/RTS or CAT PTT enabled
  • Audio Issues: Check mixer levels, device routing
  • Dupes Not Working: Ensure master.scp updated
  • Slow Performance: Close other apps, check disk space

Related Software

  • WSJT-X: FT8 modes (integrates with N1MM+)
  • fldigi: RTTY, PSK31, other digital modes
  • CQRlog: Logbook & DX spotting
  • RBN: Reverse Beacon Network spots
